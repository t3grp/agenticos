# Minimize personal and payment data

Read only fields required for the authorized accounting task. Keep tax IDs, national IDs, dates of birth, home addresses, full bank/card numbers, authentication material and identity documents out of general workpapers, chat and client/vendor drafts. Mask bank/card references to the minimum identifier needed, normally bank name and last four digits.

An authorized statutory form may require an identifier, but these general skills do not assume a form or jurisdiction and do not file it. Identify the required restricted source and reviewer workflow rather than copying identifiers into a broad review package.

Per-person payroll values belong only in the engagement's restricted payroll/tax workpapers that need them. Summarize payroll for general cash forecasts where individual names and rates are unnecessary. Exclude medical, disability, leave-reason and other unrelated sensitive details.

Customer/vendor contact details belong only with the specific draft or verification task that needs them, not in a broadly shared dashboard. No data is sent to a new recipient or copied to an external page by these skills. Use the source system's controls for sensitive details and report the accounting conclusion with the least necessary personal data.
