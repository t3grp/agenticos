# Handoffs between accounting workflows

## Preserve the measurement population

When passing a figure between skills, preserve entity, currency, period, basis, source, population and whether it is actual or forecast. A company-wide revenue figure may be context for a channel analysis but is not the denominator for that channel's return on advertising spend.

For example, a source company total of USD 971,794 and an online-store total of USD 23,653 describe different populations. Do not compute a marketing return using the whole-company numerator unless that is the explicitly supported measurement. State the numerator, denominator and scope next to every material ratio.

## Preserve workflow status

A prepared journal is not posted; a payment proposal is not scheduled or paid; a reminder draft is not sent. Pass the source identifiers, reviewer status and unresolved exceptions with the workpaper. Re-read live balances in a separately authorized execution workflow before it relies on an earlier proposal.

## Describe recurrence honestly

These accounting skills create no schedules or calendar events. Record a requested cadence, timezone, input cut-off and intended delivery as a proposed configuration for the separate scheduling workflow. Do not claim that a recurring run is active merely because a cadence was written into a draft. A completion claim requires an actual successful scheduling operation and its verified result.

## Resolve dates and timezones

Use the engagement timezone, not the server's location or an assumed calendar default. Preserve explicit offsets from source timestamps, convert to the intended timezone and flag contradictory timezone metadata. Distinguish invoice/due/settlement dates from retrieval timestamps and posting cut-offs. A future due date is expected; a future date on a supposedly completed settlement needs review.

For date-sensitive cash decisions, compare dated flows around the actual obligation date. A positive day-30 cash balance does not imply payroll can clear on day 15. Report both the relevant minimum cash date and the horizon balance.

## Carry unknowns forward

If the opening balance, payment history or population was incomplete in one skill, that limitation must remain in every downstream forecast, payment proposal and narrative. A chain must not turn a labelled assumption into an observed fact. See `absent-is-not-zero.md`.
