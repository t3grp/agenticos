# Source selection and connector neutrality

Choose sources by the engagement's evidence needs, not by connector brand. The skill does not install connectors, hold credentials or require a particular accounting product. A named product in upstream examples is not proof of a live connection.

## Source of record

Confirm which ledger owns accounting balances, which bank/treasury source establishes cash availability and which processor establishes a settlement. Two connected products may contain the same transactions. Read another source only for relevant authorized evidence and reconcile overlapping identifiers; never sum duplicate representations.

If two ledgers are in use, ask only for the missing source-of-record decision, record it and explain any reconciliation. Do not silently use whichever connector answers first. Do not mix separate tenants, legal entities, currencies, production accounts or sandbox fixtures.

## Capability is verified, not inferred

Inspect the exposed tool schema before reading. A product may support an operation that this particular connector, credential or role does not expose. Conversely, a historical upstream connector limitation may no longer apply. Describe the actual unavailable read and use supplied exports where appropriate; avoid product-wide statements such as “this ledger has no bills.”

If the same service appears through two registrations, verify account identity and authorization before choosing one. Two registrations are not two sources to add together. A refused call is a capability/access observation, not permission to bypass the connection's controls. Use an existing authorized equivalent only when it is for the same confirmed account and scope.

## Connection and spending boundaries

This pack neither creates a connection nor invokes a connector-building skill. Missing evidence is handled by a scoped export, an upload or a targeted question. Metered or paid data access requires the engagement's actual authorization; do not initiate a subscription, purchase or credential flow from a reference example.

All operations here are reads and local preparation. A product offering writes does not expand the skill's scope. Do not create, update, sync, approve, post, send, file or pay from these accounting workflows.
