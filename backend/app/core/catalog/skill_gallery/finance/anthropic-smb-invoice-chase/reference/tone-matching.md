# Reminder tone from verified history

Use the confirmed source ledger and matched payment history, normally the last twelve months. Record the observation count and definition of “on time” (receipt on or before the contractual due date). Unknown/unallocated payments do not count as late by default.

The following source-inspired thresholds are optional drafting heuristics, subordinate to the client's collection policy:

| Evidence | Draft tone |
|---|---|
| At least three resolved invoices, at least 75% paid on time | Gentle: assume an oversight without asserting why it happened. |
| At least three resolved invoices, more than 25% and at most 50% paid late | Neutral: factual follow-up without judgment. |
| At least three resolved invoices, more than 50% paid late | Firm and courteous: clear facts and a supported requested next step. |
| Fewer than three resolved invoices or incomplete history | Insufficient history: neutral wording, no adverse classification. |

Every draft includes invoice references, open amounts and ISO currencies, original due dates, days late as of the cut-off and one action request. Include only an existing verified payment link or approved instructions. A proposed response/payment date must be authorized and appropriate; do not invent legal deadlines, late fees, threats or service suspension.

Examples of subject structure: “Reminder: invoice [reference] — [currency] [amount]”; “Follow-up on overdue invoices [references]”; “Past-due balance for review — [currency] [amount].”

Consolidate multiple invoices for the same verified customer into one draft, with line-level references and totals separated by currency. Use `../shared/voice-profile.md` for an authorized style sample. Every tone produces a draft for review, never a sent message.
